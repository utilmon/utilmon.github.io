## 2. Purpose of the API Client

ArchitectureEncyclopedia Creator Upload Automation is intended solely to streamline publication of videos created and controlled by the operator.

Its primary functions may include:

- Authenticating the operator through Google OAuth 2.0.
- Selecting a local video file for upload.
- Supplying a video title and description.
- Selecting a privacy status such as `public`, `unlisted`, or `private`.
- Providing the applicable audience designation, including whether content is made for kids.
- Uploading the selected video and metadata to the operator's authorized YouTube channel.
- Reporting upload status or errors returned by YouTube API Services.

The API Client is not intended to provide access to unrelated YouTube accounts or to act as a public mass-upload service.
